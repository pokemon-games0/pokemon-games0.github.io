What is Pokémon Infinite Fusion?

Pokémon Infinite Fusion is a fan-made game centered on the idea of combining two Pokémon. Players can experiment with different pairs, explore the adventure, train their team and discover a large community-created collection of fusion designs.

How does fusion work?

The order of the two parents matters. One Pokémon can be treated as the head and the other as the body, so switching them may create a different result. That is why this page includes a Reverse Fusion button.

  • Choose two Pokémon.
  • Compare their types and base stats.
  • Try the reverse order.
  • Use Random Fusion for unexpected combinations.

About this calculator

The calculator uses public Pokémon data from PokeAPI and creates a lightweight browser preview from the two selected sprites. The displayed combined stats are averages of the parents' base stats. It is a companion tool, not an exact recreation of the game's internal fusion formulas or custom sprite library.

Custom sprites and the community

A major attraction of Infinite Fusion is its community of custom fusion artwork. Different combinations can have unique fan-created designs, while some combinations may use alternative or placeholder artwork.

Is Infinite Fusion official?

No. Pokémon Infinite Fusion is a fan-made project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo, Game Freak or The Pokémon Company. Be careful with websites claiming to be the official download source.

Frequently asked questions

Does this show the exact in-game sprite?

No. It generates a simple browser preview using public Pokémon sprites.

Can I reverse a fusion?

Yes. Use Reverse Fusion to swap the two parents.

Do I need an account?

No. The calculator runs in the browser.
