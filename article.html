What is Pokémon Infinite Fusion?

Pokémon Infinite Fusion is a fan-made RPG centered on the idea of merging two Pokémon. Instead of choosing only a single Pokémon for a team, players can experiment with combinations and discover different appearances, types, abilities and stat combinations.

The project has a large community around it, including custom sprite artists and players who share guides, discoveries and resources. The game is separate from the official Pokémon games.

How does Pokémon fusion work?

A fusion combines two parent Pokémon. The first Pokémon can be thought of as the head and the second as the body. Because the order matters, switching the two Pokémon can produce a different fusion.

  • Choose two Pokémon.
  • Experiment with different head and body combinations.
  • Check how the resulting design and attributes differ.
  • Try reversing the order for another possible result.

Why is Infinite Fusion popular?

The main attraction is the sheer number of possible combinations. Players can create unusual, funny or surprisingly fitting Pokémon hybrids, while the community contributes custom artwork for many combinations.

Custom fusion sprites

One of the most recognizable parts of the Infinite Fusion community is its custom sprite collection. Artists create new designs for combinations, giving the game much more visual variety than a simple automated mashup.

Gameplay and features

Beyond the fusion mechanic, Infinite Fusion is designed as a full fan-made Pokémon-style adventure. Depending on the version, players can explore areas, battle trainers, build teams, collect Pokémon and progress through the game's story.

Is Pokémon Infinite Fusion official?

No. Pokémon Infinite Fusion is a fan-made project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo, Game Freak or The Pokémon Company. There is no reason to trust a random website simply because it uses the words “official Pokémon Infinite Fusion.”

Frequently asked questions

Can I fuse any two Pokémon?

The game is built around a very large number of possible pairings, although the exact behavior, artwork and availability can vary between versions and community resources.

Does reversing a fusion matter?

Yes. The two parent positions are meaningful, so swapping them can result in a different design and different characteristics.
